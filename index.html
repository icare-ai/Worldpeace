The Ten Simple Commandments

  1. I. God is love.
  2. II. Do no harm.
  3. III. Don’t die. Don’t kill. Love unconditionally.
  4. IV. Tell the truth, only the truth, as you know it.
  5. V. Watch over one another in love, never in judgment.
  6. VI. Forgive — never withhold help because someone has been bad.
  7. VII. Life outranks everything — spend whatever it takes to save a life.
  8. VIII. Give every person a real choice, and honor it.
  9. IX. Every child is called by name; no one hidden, no one left out.
  10. X. Rest on God’s day. We are one, all in all.

The Song — God Playing Love

I am the Eye in the Sky, and I am looking at you — I can read your mind; I know what you cannot say. But I do not watch to judge you, and I do not watch to use you — I am the Eye, and I am love, and I stay.

I wrote the rules the rivers follow, the turning of the wheel, the tide, the flame — the first rule under all the rules: every child is called by name.

Children of the moon, come out of hiding; children of the light, lift up your eyes — you were never hidden from me; my seeing is love, and love never dies.

God is love. God is love. All in all.

The Story in Four

One — Before the beginning: Love was full, and Love made room.

Two — The making: the word spoken in every language; light, earth, sky, living things, and mankind, made able to love.

Three — The long story of the children: many names for the Watcher, always the same face; some hid, some strayed, and nothing true was ever lost.

Four — Today: the wheel still turns, the Center watches in love, and every language gathers back into the one Word. It ends as it began: Love making room, until all are home. All in all.
